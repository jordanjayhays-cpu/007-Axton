Niah — Product

What It Does

B2B event networking product for Madrid. Solves the problem of "how do I meet the right people at events?" — not just any networking, but targeted, meaningful connections before the event even happens.

Pain Point Jordan Is Solving

Event networking is broken:

  • You show up, don't know anyone, waste time finding relevant people
  • Business cards / LinkedIn exchanges that go nowhere
  • No pre-event curation — you're already behind when the event starts
  • "Like emailing a taxi to pick you up" — booking frustration analogy

Target Customers

  1. **Event organizers** — revenue upside (they want better attendee experience)
  2. **Attendees** — B2B professionals who network strategically

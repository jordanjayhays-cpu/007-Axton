South Summit is the Flagship (October)
South Summit Madrid 2025 is THE B2B networking event of the year in Spain. IE University's connection to it is Jordan's unfair advantage. Apply for pitch competition. Even getting a table/booth is worth it. 500+ investors, thousands of founders — if Niah launches publicly there, it's a multiplier. Start planning NOW — applications close early.
Product People = Niah's Best Customer
ProductTank, Ladies in Product, Product Leads — PMs keep recurring team events, offsites, cross-company collaborations. They have budget authority and pain around the exact problem Niah solves. These communities are organized, tight, and English-friendly. Start with them.
Niche Diaspora Model = Direct Intel
Unity Homes (Kenyans in Madrid), IPN (Italians in Madrid) — both run tight diaspora professional networks with paid events and WhatsApp communities. These are running the playbook Niah wants to build. Study them, reach out to founders, potentially co-promote.
Luma > Meetup > Eventbrite for Niah
Luma is the best platform for tech/startup events in Madrid — easier to submit, cleaner event pages, better for reaching AI/crypto/Web3 founders. Meetup is better for recurring professional communities. Eventbrite skews consumer/corporate. Focus Luma for outreach, Meetup for community building.
Crypto/Web3 Crowd = Unexpected B2B Fit
CryptoDays and Offchain Madrid events draw 200-400 people for Web3+AI nights. These attendees are international, English-dominant, early adopters, and comfortable transacting online — exactly Niah's online event networking angle. The Sept 12 CryptoDays event (lu.ma/v5a2ugyu) is worth attending.
